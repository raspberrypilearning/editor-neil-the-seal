## Add more costumes

Add more costumes to make **Neil**'s movement smoother.

![neil sprite](images/neil-sprite.png)

Open the **Costumes** tab. Duplicate one of your costumes to make `seal3`{:class="block3looks"}, then move the legs again for a new pose.

Keep going until **Neil** has five costumes, each with the legs in a slightly different position.

![Neil's five costumes side by side, with the legs in slightly different positions in each one](images/neil-costumes-all.png)

## Now run your code

Click the green flag and move **Neil** around.

The `next costume`{:class="block3looks"} block cycles through all five poses, giving **Neil** a smooth waddle.
